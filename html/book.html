Description

Harry Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone (also known as Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone outside the US) is the first book in J.K. Rowling’s beloved fantasy series. The story follows 11-year-old Harry Potter, who discovers on his birthday that he is a wizard. He learns about his tragic past, where his parents were killed by the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, and how he miraculously survived Voldemort’s attack as a baby. Harry attends Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he makes friends, uncovers mysteries, and faces danger. Central to the plot is the Sorcerer’s Stone, a magical object capable of granting immortality, which Voldemort seeks to regain power. With his new friends Hermione Granger and Ron Weasley, Harry unravels secrets about the stone and proves his bravery by confronting evil forces to protect it. The book is a magical tale of friendship, courage, and self-discovery that introduces readers to a richly imaginative world.
